## What shoppers said

All three Octa selectors cited its distinctive hood and generous silhouette, and two called out the fabric texture. All eleven Mira Paris selectors cited its embroidery and roomy silhouette, and ten mentioned organic cotton. Four shoppers moved away from the Octa compared with the baseline, and no shopper moved to it.
