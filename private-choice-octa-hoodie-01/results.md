## What shoppers said

Seven of the eight target selectors mentioned light weight or lightness. Five mentioned the roomier fit. Several also described temperature regulation or airflow. Columbia shoppers frequently cited its balance of price, light warmth, and pockets or easy on/off with its full zipper.

The choice review checked all 50 replies. Every stated product identity was consistent with its recorded choice. Example unsupported product-feature inferences are flagged separately. All original German text and choices were preserved.

The same adult demographic roster supplied ages and German cities. This study assigned new hooded-layer preferences and the preregistered approximate €55–€155 budgets, five shoppers at each target. Fit availability and identical purchase terms are modeled assumptions.

The Black Octa offer’s €115.92 is the exact merchant EUR display, which does not establish a delivered German checkout cost.
