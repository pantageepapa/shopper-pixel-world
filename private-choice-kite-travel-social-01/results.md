## What happened in the Reddit feed

The stock MiroFish Reddit runner started from one neutral moderator post. Each of the 50 shoppers refreshed the feed and wrote one message in each round. The shoppers authored 34 posts and 116 comments. 27 comments were on the moderator’s post, 89 were on other shoppers’ posts, and 0 were on the commenter’s own post. The database links each comment to a post. It has no nested comment-to-comment relation. The engine also recorded one like.

The 50 purchase choices came from one official private interview batch after the feed ended. All matched the SQLite interview traces and the choice named in their reasons. The prompts asked shoppers to consider the earlier discussion. No additional discussion round was run.

## How shoppers discussed the Kite and the leading offer

The Essential Kite was named in 14 of 150 shopper messages, by 14 shoppers, all in round one. The count matches either “Essential Kite” or “nozzle quiz” and excludes the moderator’s post. Eight of the 14 raised its price as a concern. Nine mentioned the pocket and four mentioned the thumb openings. Some found the pocket useful and others found it too unusual for everyday wear. Three wanted a narrower fit. These message counts describe the conversation and do not indicate how many shoppers considered buying it.
Jack Wolfskin Prelight Crew was named in 135 shopper messages, across all 50 shoppers. Among its 41 private selectors, 34 mentioned its chest pocket, 31 quick drying, 30 breathability and 24 price or budget. These features are present on the frozen Prelight card. Shopper comments about relative durability and material thickness are judgments.

## Interpretation

This run used the same 50 synthetic profiles, budgets, prices and product facts as the earlier general private eight-product comparison. The stock social feed and follow-up interviews received text cards. The earlier independent interviews used actual product images, and used a general shopping situation. The task context and feed exposure changed too, so this comparison cannot isolate any one difference. All choices are synthetic purchase intent.

The stock runner logged the configured model as `gpt-4.1-mini`. The stock response archive does not record a resolved model revision or token totals. The stock frontend’s round counter can lag in Reddit-only mode. Round completion was verified from the engine timestamps, 150 feed observations and the SQLite action records.
