## What shoppers said

Three of the four nozzle quiz selectors mentioned the mid-height shaft. Three mentioned the stated moisture- and odor-control features. Two singled out the small emblem. Many Nike reasons mentioned its €12 total price, while the three-pack attracted stated interest in price per pair or getting several pairs.

All 50 exact named choices were consistent with the product identity conveyed in the reason. Several reasons made price or pack-size mistakes, including treating the three-pack’s €10.78 per-pair price as if it met an approximate total budget. The review file flags examples and preserves every original choice and reason.

## What was verified

All 50 isolated German interviews used all five exact product photos, the matched product descriptions, the stated pack and per-pair prices, and the same purchase terms. All 250 image bytestrings matched the frozen photos by SHA-256 in the outgoing requests. The provider reported HTTP 200 for 50 distinct requests with model `gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14`. OASIS recorded 50 interviews, zero posts, and zero comments. Every product appeared ten times in each of the five positions.

The proposed shelf represents the common “Tabi Socks” search scenario. The live Zalando search grid could not be inspected. The individual competitor listings were independently sourced, and the target price came from the nozzle quiz store’s exact Ivory listing. Availability, delivery and returns were scenario assumptions. FALKE’s ordinary-toe product is included only as an adjacent alternative.

This result describes synthetic purchase intent for this specific shelf, prices, profiles and model. It does not establish how the broader German category would shop or forecast sales.
