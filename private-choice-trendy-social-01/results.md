## What happened in the Reddit feed

The stock MiroFish Reddit runner started from one neutral moderator post. Each of the 50 shoppers refreshed the feed and wrote one message in each round. The shoppers authored 39 posts and 111 comments. 40 comments were on the moderator’s post, 70 were on other shoppers’ posts, and 1 were on the commenter’s own post. The database links each comment to a post. It has no nested comment-to-comment relation.

| Round | Active shoppers | Shopper posts | Shopper comments |

The 50 purchase choices came from one official private interview batch after the feed ended. All matched the SQLite interview traces and the choice named in their reasons. The prompts asked shoppers to consider the earlier discussion. No additional discussion round was run.

## Interpretation

This run used the same 50 synthetic profiles, budgets, prices and product facts as the earlier private current-style comparison. The stock social feed and follow-up interviews received text cards. The earlier independent interviews used actual product images, so the difference cannot be attributed to social influence by itself. All choices are synthetic purchase intent.

The stock frontend’s round counter can lag in Reddit-only mode. Round completion was verified from the engine timestamps, 150 feed observations and the SQLite action records.
