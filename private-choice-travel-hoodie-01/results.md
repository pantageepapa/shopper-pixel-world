## What shoppers said

All 45 Octa selectors cited lightness and airiness. Twenty-eight explicitly mentioned compact packing, 15 mentioned the hood and eight quoted the brand’s 198 g figure. The quoted weight is a supplier specification. The shoppers’ comparative claims about weight or packed size were their inferences from the cards and photos.

## Evidence and limits

All 50 private OASIS interviews returned HTTP 200 with 15 verified image bytestrings each. SHA-256 verified all 750 transmitted image positions in their specified order. Each card occupied each of the five positions ten times. OASIS recorded 50 interview traces, zero posts and zero comments.
The exact provider model was `gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14`. Provider usage across the 50 replies was 1,772,120 input tokens and 3,994 output tokens.
The choice and reason identity review found 0 ambiguous replies. The review separately flags unsupported shopper inferences. Original German replies and choices are preserved.
The shelf includes the first four qualifying variants from the recorded Zalando search and one separately sourced Octa offer. The Octa's frozen EUR price is its merchant display. Equal delivery, returns and shopper-size availability are assumptions. Gallery angles, image resolution and background styling vary by merchant. The Octa front and back frames show more of the model and were supplied outdoors at 4417 × 5833 pixels. The Zalando gallery images were 800 pixels wide and mostly shot in a studio. The study did not isolate effects of the photography.
This separate exploratory follow-up reused the 50 baseline profiles and their preferences in fresh private sessions. It changed both the expected spend (€120–€155) and the need (a lightweight black hoodie for travel and day-to-day wear that packs compactly). Differences cannot be attributed to either change independently. Results describe these synthetic choices and do not measure real purchases or broader demand. The baseline remains reported alongside this follow-up.
