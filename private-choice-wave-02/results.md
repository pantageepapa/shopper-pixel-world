## What the five nozzle quiz selectors said

Four of five mentioned the rear pocket and four mentioned the thumb openings. Two cited quick drying and two cited recycled polyester. These are counts of explicit mentions in the five stated reasons. More than one feature can appear in the same reply.

## Conditions

Everyone browsed the same Zalando.de category scenario with the same six products, frozen prices and common purchase terms. Each isolated interview included the entire German card text, the exact matching front product image, and one decision and reason. The order was varied so each item appeared eight or nine times in each position. The roster met the planned age-band counts of 8, 15, 12, 8, 5 and 2 for ages 18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54, 55–64 and 65+, respectively. Budgets and other profile traits were synthetic design inputs.

The 50 outgoing HTTP requests were checked for six JPEG payloads in the assigned order and matched to 50 unique provider request IDs with HTTP 200 responses. Each decoded image matched the hash of its frozen source file. The provider reported 5,552–5,564 prompt tokens per response. The request audit establishes what the model received. It does not establish how much attention the model paid to any image.

The results apply to this synthetic roster, the six offered shirts, frozen scenario prices and the single model. The four-item pilot also used a different roster, prompt and budget design, so the two choice shares cannot isolate the effect of adding products or photos. The stated reasons remain as originally generated, including any assumptions a response made about fit or care.
