## What shoppers said

Five of the seven Octa selectors mentioned its hood shape, and five explicitly mentioned a roomy fit. Four mentioned breathability or temperature regulation. Twelve of the 13 Mira Paris selectors mentioned the embroidery, and 12 mentioned organic cotton. Every one of the 19 shoppers who chose none cited price. All 19 had soft budgets of €105 or lower.

The shelf includes the first four qualifying variants from the recorded Zalando search and one separately sourced Octa offer. The Octa's frozen EUR price is its merchant display. Equal delivery, returns and shopper-size availability are assumptions. Gallery angles, image resolution and background styling vary by merchant. The Octa front and back frames show more of the model and were supplied outdoors at 4417 × 5833 pixels. The Zalando gallery images were 800 pixels wide and mostly shot in a studio. This study did not isolate effects of the photography.
